# Dotyk Slov — Design v2

## North star

Dotyk Slov is not a classic Shoptet store. It should feel like a contemporary Slovak fashion/streetwear brand built around thoughts people do not always say out loud.

Core principle: **Nie všetko treba povedať nahlas.**

Visual direction: **Seed restraint × Dotyk Slov emotion × contemporary streetwear.**

## Design rules

- Quiet, editorial, flat and spacious.
- Low information density.
- Strong alternating LIGHT / DARK rhythm.
- Photography carries the emotion; UI stays restrained.
- Warm off-white instead of pure white.
- Charcoal/ink instead of harsh black.
- Dusty pink only as a small functional accent.
- Large headings use light weights (300–350), never shouty bold 700/800.
- No gradients.
- No drop shadows.
- No glassmorphism.
- Avoid excessive cards and borders.
- Pills are reserved for CTA, labels, tags and compact UI.
- Motion is subtle: reveal, fade, small horizontal movement or gentle image scale.

## Core palette

- Warm off-white: `#F7F5F0`
- Ink / charcoal: `#181817`
- Warm stone: `#ECE9E3`
- Dusty pink accent: `#E7C8CF`

## Layout

- Content max width: 1280px.
- Desktop gutters: 32px.
- Mobile gutters: 16px.
- Spacing system: 8px.
- Normal section spacing: 64–96px.
- Editorial/campaign spacing: 96–128px.

## Typography

- Primary direction: modern neutral sans-serif.
- Hero: 48–72px, weight 300–350, tight tracking.
- Section heading: 36–48px, weight 300–350.
- Subheading: 24–32px.
- Body: 16–18px.
- UI: 13–16px.

Headline principle: **whisper, not shout.**

## Homepage rhythm

1. Announcement
2. Header
3. Hero
4. Editorial intro
5. Dark product collection
6. Large campaign image
7. Category / mood navigation
8. Bestsellers
9. Brand philosophy
10. Dark editorial story
11. Community / social
12. Newsletter
13. Footer

Not every block must be present, but the homepage must feel like a story — not banner → products → banner → products.

## Homepage hero

Preferred composition:

- 40–50% content
- 50–60% photography

Content:

- small pill label
- strong but quiet headline
- short supporting sentence
- 1–2 CTAs
- dominant editorial image

Example:

`NOVÁ KOLEKCIA`

**mám toho dosť\nale esteticky**

## Product presentation

- Product image dominates.
- Desktop: usually 4 products in a row.
- Mobile: horizontal carousel with ~1.2–1.4 products visible.
- Minimal borders.
- No shadows.
- Product metadata stays compact.
- Small fashion-style labels are encouraged: `DS / 024`, `MOOD 07`, `OVERTHINKING`, `LIMITED 01`, `NEW`.

## Final filter

Before shipping any section ask:

> Does this look like a contemporary fashion brand, or Shoptet with nice CSS?

If it still feels like Shoptet, it is not finished.
